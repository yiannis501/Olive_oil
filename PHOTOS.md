# Photos the story needs

The home page tells the family's story in photos. Each slot shows a dashed "Photo needed" box until its file exists.

**How to add one:** save the photo in `public/photos/` with the exact name below (`.jpg` is fine), then push. The site uses it on the next build. To change the alt text, edit `src/photos.ts`.

| File name | Where it goes | The shot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mum-in-the-grove.jpg` | Full-screen opening | Mum walking or working among the old trees. Landscape. Step back so the whole tree is around her. Soft light, early morning or the last two hours before sunset. She does not need to look at the camera. Leave calm space in the lower left for the headline. |
| `old-trunk.jpg` | Chapter 1, beside the text | The oldest, most twisted trunk in the grove, close and filling the frame. Upright (portrait). |
| `grove-and-islands.jpg` | Chapter 1, full width | The view: trees in the front, the sea, and the Diapontian islands on the horizon. Very wide; it is cropped to a panorama. A clear day. |
| `mum-hands.jpg` | Chapter 2, beside her words | Mum's hands holding picked olives, or resting on the bark. Upright. |
| `harvest-nets.jpg` | Chapter 3, large | Harvest day: nets on the ground under a tree, people working, olives falling. Landscape. |
| `family-archive.jpg` | Chapter 3, small | An old family photo taken in the grove. Photograph the print flat, in daylight, without flash. |
| `at-the-table.jpg` | Chapter 4 | The family table: bread, a bowl of oil, a meal. Hands and food, not posed faces. Upright. |

## Tips

- A recent phone is good enough. Turn off portrait mode and filters.
- Shoot more than you need: 20 to 30 of each scene, and pick the best.
- Send the largest original file (at least 2400 px wide). Do not send screenshots or WhatsApp copies, which are compressed.
- Ask Mum before any photo of her goes online.

## Words only you can write

The page also has yellow highlighted gaps, marked with `<Fill>` in `src/pages/index.astro`:

- Mum's name
- The village near the grove
- The harvest months
- Two or three true sentences about Mum and the grove
- Something Mum says about the trees, in her own words
